Retracted by arithmetic

Austin Wagner

A result was withdrawn without any later experiment disagreeing with it. Counting the question set was enough to show that no outcome of that run could ever have supported what it reported.

Retraction dated 2026-09-09. Every figure below names the section of the research record it comes from; the source-verification block at the end carries the chain.

The claim

A question-answering benchmark reported that a trained KV-cache cartridge — a corpus compiled into a model's attention state rather than retrieved into its prompt — beats lexical, dense and fused retrieval from roughly 774M parameters.

It was the headline finding of that programme. It is withdrawn.

The arithmetic, which needed no new measurement

The reported differences were 0.0521 and 0.0417 in accuracy over a 32-item question set. In items, that is 1.7 and 1.3.

The comparison is McNemar's test at an alpha of 0.05 with the mid-p correction. That test's verdict depends only on the pairs where the two arms disagree, and the fewest discordant pairs that can ever reject the null is five. A difference of 1.3 items cannot produce five discordant pairs in the required direction.

No split of that question set could have supported the claim. There was no unlucky sample and no error in the analysis. The measurement was built such that the finding it reported was unreachable.

What survives, which is a different comparison

The same run scored the cartridge against the un-augmented base as well, and there it moved 8.7 and 8.3 items — above the five-pair floor and above the arm's own seed spread.

Two claims came out of one run and only one of them is supportable, which is the ordinary case rather than an awkward one. Retracting the whole run would have thrown away a real result. Softening the unsupportable half to "suggests" would have kept an unreachable claim in circulation wearing a hedge.

The same question, answered in the opposite direction

A separate run over 12 pages of the same corpus, scoring 24 held-out items, reported the reverse: the cartridge losing to lexical retrieval, 0.6389 against 0.8333. That run's own write-up recorded its cartridge gain as falling inside its seed spread, so it was candid about half of its result. But 24 items sits further below the five-pair floor than the 32-item set that produced the retracted claim.

An instrument too weak to resolve a difference will still emit one, with a sign, and the sign is noise. Two readings of the same question came off the same class of apparatus and pointed opposite ways.

The useful observation is not that one of the two directions happened to be right. It is that neither run could distinguish its answer from the other one.

The repair is a refusal, not a better analysis

A plan now declares the smallest effect worth detecting, the alpha, and the test. The run is refused before the model loads when the question set it will actually score cannot resolve what the plan declares. Three properties do the work:

  • It reads the realised count, never the configured cap. The 32-item set came from a plan whose cap said 120. A gate reading the cap would have passed it.
  • It is a falsifiability gate, not a power one. Passing means some attainable outcome supports the declared effect. It does not mean that outcome is likely, and classifying an observed result against the disagreements that actually occurred is a separate statement.
  • It refuses rather than warns. The plans that produced the retracted claim are among the refused set, deliberately.

Why it went unnoticed

The command carried the programme's headline result while appearing in 0 of 571 committed run documents, 0 lines of the research index, and 0 tracked artifacts. Its records went to a temporary directory that is purged, and it ran on a local workstation where nothing requires a container digest or a staged corpus.

Nothing checked. A guard rule now fails the lint on any entry point that builds a run record and is named nowhere in the index.

What is not yet known

A replacement measurement is registered: 3,740 items over an 841-page corpus, a resolvable floor of 0.00134 against a declared effect of 0.02, across base, cartridge, oracle, lexical, dense, fused, expanded, reranked and long-context arms, three seeds, on one A100.

Its results are not in the committed record. Four jobs were submitted and their artifacts sit on cluster storage, so whether a properly powered instrument reproduces either direction is currently unknown. The question is open — a third state, distinct from both answers previously given for it.
