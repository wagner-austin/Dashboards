Every card is one generated sprite module, played at its real settings.animation interval from config.json — this is exactly what the engine loads.

Onion skin draws the previous frame underneath the live one. Registration drift (the character sliding or resizing between frames) shows up as a doubled outline.

Churn is the share of occupied cells that change between consecutive frames. It is not an absolute quality score — the 5-character gradient flips a cell on any small brightness change, so the shipped bunny sprites already span 15% (idle) to 85% (the 2-frame turns). Each card prints the matching bunny figure beside it, and the colour is the gap: green within 5 points of the bunny baseline, amber within 15, red beyond. A new character whose walk churns far above the bunny's 70% is being redrawn each frame rather than animated. Churn highlight marks the changed cells in red inside the live frame, which is where you see which parts are drifting.
