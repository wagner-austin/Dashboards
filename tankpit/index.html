    How does it decide what to do?

    A hierarchical finite-state machine separates the bot’s goal from the steps needed to carry it out.

    Collect supplies

    Sense → Search → Approach → Pick up

    Scan for fuel and equipment, choose a route, then collect what it needs.

    Hunt an opponent

    Find → Approach → Engage → Confirm

    Track an enemy, get into range, fight and check the outcome.

    Fuel and equipment determine when it switches goals. It remembers targets between turns and waits for the game to confirm its commands.

    New to TankPit?

    TankPit is a multiplayer tank game. The goal is to keep your tank supplied while fighting other tanks.

    • Fuel keeps the tank alive and pays for teleports.
    • Radar reveals nearby supplies and mines.
    • Equipment includes armour and special shots.

    This bot reads the game's messages and sends its own commands. The browser shows you the result. These demos stay in the practice room and do not affect ranked play.
